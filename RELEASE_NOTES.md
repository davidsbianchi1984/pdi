**PDI v0.9.0** — **no functional change to the vault in this release**:
the three products are cut as one release, and the version moves so one
number keeps naming one combination of all three.

### What changed in the siblings

JIM-mini's medicine cabinet: medications in the user's own words, a day
board with humane grace, an as-needed ceiling that refuses to log past
itself, and a coach that notices a missed critical dose without ever
alarming.

### Verification

266 tests green, unchanged in behaviour — which is the point.

### Install

If you have 0.7.0 or later, this arrives on its own — one restart when prompted.
Otherwise, download the installer for your OS from the assets below, or
run `python -m pdi`. Deployed on-premises or in colocation — your
hardware, your keys (`PDI_MASTER_KEY`), your walls.
